Invictus Energy ready to roll in Zimbabwe with Musuma-1 exploration well spud looming

  • Well services equipment mobilisation commenced for upcoming Musuma-1 exploration well
  • Rig-up operations underway following successful relocation of Exalo Rig 202 from Mukuyu-2 wellsite

Invictus Energy (ASX: IVZ) is on target to spud the much-awaited Musuma-1 exploration well in Zimbabwe with preparations progression for the drilling in the Cabora Bassa Basin area.

Exalo Rig 202 has moved across from Mukuyu-2 and is being assembled at Musuma-1, while well services crews and gear arrive in stages and final maintenance, installation and testing continue before the rig is formally accepted.

Managing director Scott Macmillan said the team is concentrating on finishing rig-up and bringing in the last of the equipment, with the spud expected within weeks depending on when critical well services gear reaches Zimbabwe.

He described the well as a chance to probe a major new target that could add to the company's existing gas-condensate finds in the basin.

Musuma-1 tests a new play type, aiming at a prospect estimated to hold 1.2 TCF of gas and 73 million barrels of condensate, flagged by a seismic direct hydrocarbon indicator (DHI).

It is one of eight promising prospects mapped on seismic, and a success could open up a large new resource alongside the proven Mukuyu gas field.

The earlier Mukuyu-1 and Mukuyu-2 discoveries put Invictus on the world stage, with Wood Mackenzie ranking Mukuyu as sub-Saharan Africa's second largest find of 2023.

Shares in IVZ fell 7.25% to 6.4¢, for a market capitalisation of $123.2M.
